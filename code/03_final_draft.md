# Cuffing Season
Connor Mahone

Cuffing Season is the name ascribed to a phenomenon in which people seek
out romantic partnership during the cold months of a year. Cuffing
Season is well-documented and is even described in the Merriam Webster
Dictionary as “a period of time where single people begin looking for
short term partnerships to pass the colder months of the year.” This
begs the questions:

- Is cuffing season a real trend?

- Does it transcend age groups?

- Are couples that meet during cuffing season doomed to fail?

So while it appears that cuffing season is a real trend that exists
among young people, this is not newly established. Dr. Lehmiller for
Vice
[reported](https://www.vice.com/en/article/what-is-cuffing-season-winter-relationships-commitment/)
that online dating peaks in the colder months. Cuffing season is often
linked with an air of finiteness, the relationships that start during
cuffing season are said to be short-term.

So if relationships that start during the cold months begin more
hastily, does that mean those relationships are destined to end?

Table: There is little variation in the success rate of a relationship
by the month the couple met

Survey Data from 2017 of American Adults from Stanford’s How Couples
Meet and Stay Together 2017 (HCMST 2017). Season Met Partner is derived
from the month each recipient met their partner, grouped by the
meteorolical season. Weighted \# of Instances is the number of partners
that met in that season, controlled for oversampling. Weighted \# of
Break Ups is the number of those partners that were no longer together
at the time of the survey, controlled for oversampling. Ratio of Break
Ups to Instances is the ratio of Weighted \# of Break Ups to Weighted \#
of Instances

| Season Met Partner | Weighted \# of Instances | Weighted \# of Break Ups | Ratio of Break Ups to Instances |
|---:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| Winter | 648.9077 | 74.9054 | 0.1154 |
| Spring | 789.1640 | 91.2442 | 0.1156 |
| Summer | 1043.2181 | 106.9878 | 0.1026 |
| Fall | 829.8106 | 84.7804 | 0.1022 |

The data suggests that even though cuffing season is a real trend, and
people who meet their partners during those months commit to a
relationship quicker, there is no significant difference in the success
rates of relationships by the season.
